# Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Template

## Context

What is the architectural issue that needs to be addressed?

## Decision

What is the chosen solution and why?

## Status

What is the status of this decision? (Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated, Superseded)

## Consequences

What are the positive and negative consequences of this decision?

## Alternatives Considered

What other solutions were considered?

## Tags

What tags categorize this ADR? (e.g., infrastructure, security, ui, api)

## Related

What other ADRs, specs, or documents are related to this decision?
